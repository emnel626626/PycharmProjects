Text-The-Resident

How to use the daily General Surgery (ABSITE) quiz

What it is

Every morning at 7:00 AM you get one multiple-choice question by text, in the style of the ABSITE, with topics weighted like the real exam (trauma, critical care, breast, endocrine and the rest of the ABS outline). Reply with your answer and you get the right answer and a short explanation right away. It takes about a minute a day.

Getting started

Save this number as Text-The-Resident, then text START to it:

(839) 225-4294

  1. Text START to the number above.
  2. When it asks for your first name, reply with it.
  3. Your first question arrives right away. After that, a new one comes every morning.

If you're already getting the daily questions, you're set. There's nothing to do.

Answering a question

Reply with just the letter: A, B, C or D. You'll get back whether you were right, the correct answer if you weren't, and an explanation of why.

  • Questions are short clinical vignettes, like the ABSITE. Pick the single best answer.
  • Answer whenever you like. The question stays open until you reply.
  • If you get "Please reply with A, B, C, or D," you have a question waiting, so just send the letter.

The leaderboard

Everyone is on the leaderboard by first name, ranked by total correct answers, with ties going to the higher accuracy. Text BOARD to see the top 5 and where you stand. If you'd rather not be listed, text BOARD OFF. Your answers still count; you just won't show up.

Good to know

  • Questions are written and double-checked by AI, so the odd one may still be wrong. If an answer looks off, text FLAG so it gets reviewed.
  • You'll get about one text a day, plus replies to what you send. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Text STOP anytime to unsubscribe.
  • No reply, or something not working? Text HELP, or ask Dr. Chris Strader.
