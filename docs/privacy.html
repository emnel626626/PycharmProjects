Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Text-The-Resident ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS quiz service.

Information We Collect

  • Your mobile phone number, when you text START to opt in.
  • The content of messages you send us (e.g. your quiz answers A/B/C/D, or keywords like STOP/HELP).

How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number and message content solely to deliver quiz questions, process your answers, send feedback/explanations, and respond to opt-out or help requests. We do not use this information for any other purpose.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your information may be shared with the SMS carriers and messaging platform providers (e.g. Twilio) solely as needed to deliver the service.

Data Retention

We retain your phone number and quiz progress only for as long as needed to operate the service, or until you opt out by replying STOP.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the Text-The-Resident program contact.
